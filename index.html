01 / Our Story

Since 2000.
A quiet, consistent presence in education and industry.

Established in 2000, Rajguru Book Manufacturers was founded with a clear purpose to manufacture reliable, high-quality notebooks that meet the everyday needs of students, professionals, and institutions. What began as a focused manufacturing initiative has grown into a trusted name in the paper products segment, serving customers across cities, towns, and various regions.

For over two decades, we have been a quiet but consistent presence in classrooms, offices, and workplaces. Our notebooks are designed for regular use, emphasizing strong paper quality, durable binding, and dependable performance qualities that have earned the confidence of our customers year after year.

Our manufacturing capabilities extend across school and college notebooks, as well as customized stationery solutions for offices and organizations. Every product reflects our commitment to consistency, quality control, and responsible manufacturing practices.

“To manufacture with integrity, consistency, and care.”
— Rajguru Book Manufacturers Guiding Principle

01

Select & Inspect

SOURCE

We source high-brightness, woodfree, high-GSM paper reels from certified sustainable paper mills. Every lot is checked for opacity, grain direction, and ink holdout.
02

Formulate & Print

RULE

Continuous high-speed web ruling ensures crisp lines, razor-sharp red margin borders, and perfect register accuracy across single line, four-line, and square grid sheets.
03

Collate & Fold

GATHER

Automated cross-folding and precision collating assemble the exact verified page counts with preface index sheets, student identity grids, and syllabus dividers.
04

Stitch & Reinforce

BIND

Built to last: heavy-gauge galvanized wire stitching for soft bounds, durable spine gluing, and heavy-duty calico cloth tape spines for commercial accounting registers.
05

Trim & Deliver

DISPATCH

Computerized three-knife edge trimming yields glass-smooth edges. Bundles are sealed in heavy protective shrink-wrap and shipped to distributors across cities and towns.
